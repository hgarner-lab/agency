# UNDERGROUND sneaker art direction

Four generated product renders based on the shoes worn in `assets/crew.png`. Each is a separate original shoe design in the existing crew's colour palette. Generated with the built-in image generator, using the crew image as a visual reference and requesting transparency. Original renders were optimised to 960×640 WebP with alpha preserved for the game.

## lime

Final asset: `assets/trainer-lime-v1.webp`

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: transparent sneaker product cutout for the UNDERGROUND youth agency game. Input image is a STYLE and SHOE DESIGN REFERENCE showing the existing four game characters. Create ONE beautifully detailed premium 3D sneaker, taking the specific trainer on the named character as the design starting point. Match the polished stylized 3D materials, physical chunky proportions and premium rendering of that character sheet. Cool London streetwear, technical 2000s runner meets sculpted chunky trail sneaker. Convincing sculpted outsole, layered textile and leather panels, mesh, threaded laces, tongue, realistic seams. No cartoon outlines. Composition: one isolated RIGHT-facing shoe, toe to the right and heel to the left, low three-quarter lateral product angle, see outer side, toe and a little top, complete shoe centered with even margins, wide landscape composition about 3:2, shoe fills 85% of frame horizontally. Same low camera angle across the collection. Lighting: soft high-end studio keylight and gentle rimlight, clear premium materials and depth. Background fully transparent with clean alpha, no backdrop, no checkerboard, no platform or pedestal, minimal contact shadow only if transparent. No people, no legs, no extra shoe, no text, no labels, no logos, no watermark, no brand marks. Colourway specification: REM, leftmost character. Anthracite black layered upper, acid lime green laces and mesh accents, chunky black sole with large lime green tread pods and toe bumper. Angular tactical tech-runner with a deep rugged tread, like the trainers worn by REM in the reference.
