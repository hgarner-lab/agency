# UNDERGROUND identity: colour UG

## Final identity

Primary mark: a flat lime U (`#c6ff40`) and lilac G (`#b79cf5`). The full UNDERGROUND name sits underneath in warm white in the full lockup. Angular, forward-slanted letterforms are built as clean SVG paths. There is no underline or stepped-G detail.

Files:
- `assets/underground-logo.svg`: full lockup, transparent background.
- `assets/underground-monogram.svg`: compact two-letter mark, transparent background.
- `src/underground-brand.js`: matching inline paths for campaign artwork.
- `assets/client-jamie-ug-v2.webp`: Jamie with the compact UG mark on the shoebox.

The full name uses outlines from the existing Nimbus Sans Narrow Bold game font; the included font licence remains in `assets/FONT-LICENSE.txt`. The UG letterforms are drawn as paths.

## Placement in the experience

1. **Packaging:** a small physical label on Jamie's shoebox gives the mark a natural first appearance.
2. **Brief:** the compact mark sits beside UNDERGROUND in the client brief header. The price, audience, trust problem and deadline keep their existing prominence.
3. **Campaign output:** a small brand signature appears on the poster and billboard; the social post uses UG as the profile avatar. This connects the students' work to the client.

The experience header, input controls and role descriptions retain their existing identity. McCann continues to brand the downloadable role cards. The mark is static and has no extra interactions. Existing gameplay, task copy, moodboard backdrop and facilitator flow are preserved.

## Image workflow

An initial colour-logo concept was made with the built-in image generator. Its raster alpha edges were unsuitable for small web use; the shipped logos are clean SVG artwork. Jamie's packaging was edited with the built-in image generator, preserving alpha during optimisation to WebP. The final packaging uses only UG, with no tiny wordmark.

### Colour-logo exploration prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create the final standalone transparent logo asset for UNDERGROUND, the fictional sneaker brand. Use the supplied identity board ONLY as a reference for the bold, forward-slanted, angular UG letter shapes in the top-right. The new primary logo is simply a very large UG monogram, with the letter U in flat acid lime #c6ff40 and the letter G in flat lilac purple #b79cf5. Two solid letter colours, no gradients or textures. The simple colour difference must be immediately obvious. Use robust familiar readable U and G shapes from the board, no experimental stepped notch. Beneath UG place the full word UNDERGROUND in much smaller bold white uppercase condensed sans-serif lettering, spelled exactly, centered and aligned to the width of the UG. Maintain generous separation between monogram and small wordmark. Monogram takes about 80% of the logo height, small wordmark 12%. Precise flat professional vector-style artwork, very crisp clean edges, centred and tightly framed with a modest even margin. Transparent background with actual alpha, no black rectangle, no photograph, no shoebox, no shoe, no board, no backdrop, no shadow, no extra symbol, no underline, no rule, no badge, no slogan or other text. ONE logo lockup only: lime U + lilac G, white UNDERGROUND underneath. Logo should work on the dark surfaces in the game. Horizontal 4:3 frame or near-square frame.
